## Long Description (shared, ≤4000 chars)

SleepRings is an ambient sound mixer where you place sounds directly on orbits around a center point to build your own sleep soundscape.

**Place sound on an orbit**
The center of the screen is you. Drag a sound token anywhere and it starts orbiting from that spot. The closer to the center, the louder it plays; the farther out, the quieter. Its angle shapes the stereo image — left, right, front, back. Listen with headphones for the full binaural spatial effect.

**Shape the orbit with your fingers**
- Hold a sound in place to lock its position
- Pinch to squeeze its orbit into an ellipse, or spread to round it back out
- Drag it to the bottom of the screen to remove it
- Tap it to open volume and lock controls

**Four noise generators**
White, pink, brown, and green noise, synthesized in real time. Layer them together to build your own background texture.

**Through the night and beyond**
Set a sleep timer and the mix fades out gently before playback stops. Name and save any layout as a preset, and load it back anytime.

**Never loses your night**
Your mix auto-saves, so reopening the app picks up right where you left off. Playback works without a network connection.

**Free, and ad-free**
15 sounds plus orbit mixing, the sleep timer, presets and auto-dim are free. Ads appear only as a small banner on the settings screen — **never on the screen you actually listen on**. Ten more sounds unlock with a subscription.
