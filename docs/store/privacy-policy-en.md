SleepRings ("the App") has no sign-up, login, or account, and sends no user data to any server of its own. The App does use the Google AdMob SDK to show banner ads; what that SDK processes for ad purposes is disclosed separately below under "Analytics and Advertising SDKs".

## Information We Collect

The App itself collects no personal information. However, the banner ad SDK (Google AdMob) processes advertising identifiers (iOS: IDFA, Android: Advertising ID) and device/network information for ad delivery and measurement — this is collected and processed by Google directly, not by the App.

## Information Stored on Your Device
- **Subscription tier**: your selected plan (free or paid tier) is stored on the device only. Payments are handled by the App Store / Google Play; this app never receives or stores payment details.


The following is stored only in local storage on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

- Your current mix (placed sounds' positions, volumes, etc.)
- Saved presets
- Language and other settings

Uninstalling the App deletes this data along with it.

## Analytics and Advertising SDKs

The App includes the **Google AdMob** SDK to show banner ads (no full-screen or rewarded video ads are used). AdMob collects and processes advertising identifiers, approximate location (country/region level), device information, and app interaction data for ad delivery and measurement; this data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

- **iOS**: an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is shown on first launch. Declining does not limit App functionality; non-personalized ads are shown instead.
- **EEA/UK users**: a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow (GDPR) applies.
- The App does not collect directly identifying information (name, email, contact details), and includes no analytics or tracking SDK other than AdMob.

## Network Access

The App's core functionality (playing sound mixes, saving presets) works fully offline, with no account server or API calls. Displaying banner ads does require the AdMob SDK to request ads over the network.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not use child-directed ad settings. Users under the applicable age of consent should not use the App without a parent or guardian's permission.
