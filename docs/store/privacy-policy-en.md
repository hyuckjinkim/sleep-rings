SleepRings ("the App") does not collect personal information.

## Information We Collect

None. The App has no sign-up, login, or account, and sends no user data to any server.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only in local storage on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

- Your current mix (placed sounds' positions, volumes, etc.)
- Saved presets
- Language and other settings

Uninstalling the App deletes this data along with it.

## Analytics and Advertising SDKs

The App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not track your behavior or share data with third parties.

## Network Access

The App works fully offline. There is no account server or API calls.

## Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, no personal information from children is collected or processed regardless of age.
